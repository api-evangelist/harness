The March 2026 drone strikes on AWS data centers in the UAE and Bahrain — the first confirmed military attack on a hyperscale cloud provider — exposed how unprepared many organisations are for a real regional cloud failure. The blog argues that having a DR plan is not enough; it must be tested regularly under realistic conditions. Harness Resilience Testing addresses this by enabling organisations to simulate AWS region failures and run end-to-end DR test workflows, making rigorous DR testing efficient, repeatable, and frequent. | Blog
